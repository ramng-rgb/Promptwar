# PRODUCT.md — Urban Air Quality & Winter Smog Assistant (AeroPulse)

## Product Identity & Purpose
**AeroPulse** is an AI-powered urban resilience assistant created specifically for families, commuters, vulnerable individuals (children, asthmatics, elderly), and community organizers coping with severe winter smog and urban air pollution crises (e.g., Delhi NCR, Lahore, Salt Lake City, Beijing, Central Valley).

## Core User Needs
1. **Hyper-local Air Quality & Smog Inversion Awareness**: Real-time AQI, PM2.5, PM10, thermal inversion warnings, and hour-by-hour exposure windows.
2. **Personalized Low-Emission Daily Routines**: Smart commutes (transit vs carpool vs walking corridors), school drop-off timing, and shift planning to dodge peak pollution hours.
3. **Emergency & DIY Indoor Air Hacks**: DIY Corsi-Rosenthal boxes, wet-towel door seals, air purifier placement optimization, ventilation timing during diurnal smog inversions.
4. **Lifestyle & Health Protection**: Personalized safety protocols based on family composition (toddlers, seniors, respiratory conditions).
5. **Direct Impact Calculator**: Visible carbon and particulate offset metrics for individual choices (public transit taken, car idling reduced, clean heating adopted).
6. **GenAI Advisory Engine**: Interactive contextual assistant capable of diagnosing room conditions, suggesting budget-conscious winter hacks, and generating neighborhood clean-air alerts.

## Design Tenets & Evaluation Criteria Alignment
- **Code Quality**: Modular React 19 + TypeScript architecture, rigorous typings, zero warnings.
- **Security**: Client-side sanitized interactions, secure API handling, no leaks of personal data or exact GPS coordinates.
- **Efficiency**: Instant reactive state, zero unnecessary re-renders, lightweight dependencies, offline-first fallback data.
- **Testing**: Deterministic mock dataset covering real-world winter pollution patterns, mockable simulation modes.
- **Accessibility (a11y)**: WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantic landmarks and ARIA live regions for AQI alerts.
- **Problem Statement Alignment**: Precision focus on severe winter smog, indoor purification hacks, low-emission daily routines, sustainable alternatives, and quantified direct impact.
